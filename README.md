# 🌍 Stilt Safaris

A professional tourism and safari website designed and developed for **Stilt Safaris**, a Tanzania-based tourism and safari business.

The website was created to provide travelers with an informative, engaging, and user-friendly online platform for discovering safari experiences, destinations, and tourism services in Tanzania.

---

## 🌐 Live Website

🔗 **[Visit Stilt Safaris](https://stiltsafari.com/)**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Stilt Safaris is a tourism website designed to showcase safari experiences, travel destinations, and tourism services in Tanzania.

The project focuses on creating a professional digital presence for the tourism business while making it easier for potential travelers to discover safari experiences and access relevant travel information.

The website was designed with a focus on:

* Professional visual presentation
* Responsive web design
* User-friendly navigation
* Tourism and safari content
* Clear presentation of travel experiences
* Customer communication and inquiries

## 🎯 Project Goals

The main goals of the project were to:

* Create a professional online presence for Stilt Safaris.
* Showcase Tanzania's safari destinations and travel experiences.
* Make tourism information easy for potential travelers to access.
* Provide a responsive experience across different devices.
* Present safari and tourism services in a clear and attractive way.
* Make it easier for potential customers to contact the business.
